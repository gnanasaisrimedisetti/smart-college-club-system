Set Operations Lab

Use mathematical set operations to compare club memberships.

Union · A ∪ B

—Members in A or B or both

Intersection · A ∩ B

—Members common to both

Difference · A − B

—Only in club A

Difference · B − A

—Only in club B

What the operations mean

Union (A ∪ B): students who belong to at least one of the selected clubs.

Intersection (A ∩ B): students who are members of both clubs.

Difference (A − B): students who belong to A but not B. B − A works in the opposite direction.

Event eligibility: a student qualifies when they are a member of every club selected as required for that event.
